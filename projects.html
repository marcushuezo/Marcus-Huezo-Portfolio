My Projects

Networking Project - Intro to Networking 2

I built a simulated network for CryptoMine, connecting their offices in Chicago, Toronto, Vancouver and Mexico using 6 routers and 9 switches. I configured VLANs, routing and DNS, tested connections between the offices and set up SSH for secure remote management from Ontario Tech Residence.

AGF Investments - Intern Project

During my internship, I worked on a case study exploring how AGF could improve client loyalty during market volatility by learning from other industries. I spoke with people within the firm and researched how other industries support clients during difficult situations. I recommended timely updates and regular follow-ups to keep clients informed, along with solutions such as more YouTube videos featuring analysts discussing their specific sectors rather than only providing general market updates to keep clients more informed.

Wordie - Intro to Programming for IT

For a group assignment, we built a Python game where players have six attempts to guess a randomly selected five letter word. It checks guesses and provides feedback on a digital keyboard to show letters already guessed. After each round, it displays the correct word and its definition and tracks wins, losses, win percentage and winning streaks.

Car Rental System - Object-Oriented Programming

I worked on a Python car rental system that manages vehicles, customers, rentals and returns. It uses classes and inheritance to calculate rental costs, availability of the vehicles, and apply late fees when vehicles are not returned on time. The program saves customer, vehicle and, rental data in CSV files.
